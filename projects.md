# 🚀 Projects

Explore a collection of engineering projects I've completed throughout high school, undergraduate studies, and my master's degree.

---

## 🏫 High School Projects

- [Mouse-Trap Powered Cart 🐭](projects/mousetrap-cart/)  
  Where the mechanics bug first bit. A stored-energy vehicle built in Grade 11 that took 3rd place in the 2015 time rankings for the fastest mousetrap-powered cart.

## 🎓 Undergraduate Projects

- [Skip-the-Servers 🍽️](projects/capstone/)  
  Capstone project: an autonomous food delivery robot aimed at the staffing pressures restaurants face, combining robotics with intelligent automation.

- [CATIA V5R19 – GM Engine Reverse Engineering 🛠️](projects/catia-v5r19/)  
  Hand-disassembled a General Motors Eco-Tec-4 engine at the McMaster Automotive Research Centre, then measured, modelled, and animated the full assembly in CATIA V5R19.

- [Fusion 360 Gear-Driven Bike 🚴‍♂️](projects/fusion-bike/)  
  A gear-driven bicycle designed in Autodesk Fusion 360, built around a lightweight, modern frame concept.

## 🎓 Master's Projects

- [Autonomous Vehicle Perception System 🚗](projects/gowrench-autonomy/)  
  Developed with GoWrench Inc.: an off-road autonomous robot integrating GNSS, ROS, and YOLO-based vision to support Level 4 navigation.

- [Human Pose Estimation 🧍‍♂️](projects/pose-estimation/)  
  A comparison of leading pose estimation models for real-time human keypoint detection, evaluated on accuracy and practical runtime behaviour.

- [MQTT Anomaly Detection 📡](projects/mqtt-anomaly/)  
  Deep learning applied to IoT security: detecting anomalies in MQTT sensor streams using time-series models and ensemble methods.
